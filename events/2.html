Event Details

Overview

In the Sumo Bot event, competitors pit robots against eachother with the goal of pushing your opponent's robot out of a ring. Whoever manages to push their opponent out first wins the game!

Rules

  • Two robots start on opposite sides of the ring.
  • The goal is to push the opponent out of the ring while staying inside.
  • Robots should use the force sensor to detect impact and respond by increasing power or changing strategy.
  • A robot loses if:
    • Any part of it touches outside the ring.
    • It remains inactive for more than 10 seconds.
  • Best of three rounds determines the winner.
